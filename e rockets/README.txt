MAGIC E ROCKET — Reading Express
Open index.html in a browser.

Mechanic:
1. A CVC word appears on the rock.
2. The magic-e rocket slowly descends beside it.
3. When the rocket reaches the word, the magic-e word appears.
4. Tap the rocket: it launches upward, the word melts away, and progress increases.
5. 12 meaningful pairs are shuffled each game.

Pairs:
cap→cape, can→cane, kit→kite, pin→pine, fin→fine, rid→ride,
hop→hope, cop→cope, rob→robe, tub→tube, cub→cube, cut→cute.
